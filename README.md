# News

A calm news reader made for E Ink. You choose the sources, and there is no account and no feed algorithm.

[![ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/img/githubbutton_sm.svg)](https://ko-fi.com/tintako)

## What it can do

- **Today:** the newest stories from all the sources you follow
- **Following:** manage your sources
- **Add source:** on Following, tap the black + and type a site's link or its RSS link; the app finds the feed
- **QR scan:** send a link from your computer by scanning it, with a flashlight for dark rooms
- **RSS links:** other apps can open RSS and Atom links with News; you follow the source with a tap on +
- **Clean reader:** articles without the clutter around them
- **Reading options:** text size, Lato or serif font, and a switch for pictures
- **Saved:** keep stories for offline reading
- **Offline:** shows when the news was last updated

**Languages:** English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Czech

## Permissions
- Internet: to download feeds, articles and pictures
- Camera: only to scan a QR code, while the scanner is open; asked once, and you can allow it later in Settings
