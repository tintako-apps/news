# News · Privacy Policy

Developer: Tintako · Croatia · app@tintako.com
Effective 21 November 2026. This policy says the same as Privacy in the app (Settings → About → Privacy).

## The short version
News collects no personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reports, and it doesn't use Google services. Your data never reaches Tintako, and nothing is shared with third parties.

## Who is responsible
Tintako, app@tintako.com. Tintako runs no servers for the app and receives no data from it.

## What the app stores, and where
Only on your phone, in the app's private storage: the sources you follow, which stories you've read, your saved stories (their text and, if pictures are on, their pictures) and your settings. The app is left out of phone backups, so none of it is copied to a cloud. Uninstall the app or clear its data and everything is gone.

If you use Tintako Launcher, its News card shows your three newest headlines. Only Tintako Launcher, on the same phone, can read the card; the headlines don't leave the phone.

## Permissions, and what each is for
- **Internet:** to download feeds, articles and pictures.
- **Camera:** only to scan a QR code. The first time you open the scanner, the app asks once whether to turn it on; after Later it doesn't ask again, and you can allow it under Settings → General. It's used only while the scanner is open. Camera images are never saved or sent. If you decline, everything else works.

## What leaves, and only when you ask
- When you add a source by its link or by scanning, or open an RSS link with the app from another app, the app contacts that site to find its feed. The source is followed only when you tap +.
- When the news refreshes (when you open the app and the news is more than 30 minutes old, or when you tap refresh in the app or on the News card in Tintako Launcher), the app downloads the feeds of the sources you follow. A feed that hasn't changed isn't downloaded again.
- When you open a story, the app downloads that page and its picture, if pictures are on.

Requests go straight from your phone to those sites, over a secure connection whenever the site offers one. They carry no cookies and no Referer, every request asks the site not to track you (Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track), tracking tags such as utm_ and fbclid are removed from links, and none of the site's scripts, trackers or ads run.

## Who else receives anything
- **The news sites you add:** they see your IP address when the app downloads their feed or an article, just as when you visit them.
- **Picture servers:** the picture at the top of a story often comes from a different server, which also sees your IP address. Turn pictures off under Settings → General to contact fewer servers.

No one else receives anything. Every server the app contacts sees your IP address, as with any visit to a website; hiding it takes a VPN.

## Legal basis (EU/EEA and UK)
Tintako doesn't process your personal data, so it needs no legal basis for processing. The sites you choose to follow process your IP address under their own privacy policies when the app contacts them at your request.

## Your rights
Tintako holds no data about you, so there is nothing to hand over, correct or delete. Everything the app keeps is on your phone and you can remove it at any time. You can complain to your national data protection authority. Questions: app@tintako.com.

## Children
The app has no accounts and collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
This policy may change with a new app version. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
